## Found
- A per-lesson lexicon is the only honest guard for vocabulary, and literals (backticks, quoted names, tokens with non-letters, NameError-style identifiers) are not words.
- YAML reads `yes`, `no` and `on` as booleans.
- Tool messages differ by version and shell:
  - uutils and GNU print different `mkdir` messages;
  - git's outside-a-repository message depends on the mount point;
  - bash's "command not found" differs between interactive and non-interactive shells.
- Real help pages hold far more words than an A2 learner knows.
